Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Zero Cee ("we," "us," or "our") operates the zerocee.ch website. This page explains our privacy practices, the legal basis for processing, and how we handle your personal data when you use our website and booking form. We are the data controller for the purposes of the GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a booking inquiry through our form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Event type and date
  • Venue/location information
  • Any additional details or messages you provide

This information is collected only when you voluntarily submit it. We do not collect any personal data from visitors who simply browse the site without submitting the form.

Server & hosting logs: This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like virtually all web hosts, GitHub's servers automatically record standard connection logs for every visitor (IP address, browser/user-agent string, referring page, and timestamp) for security and abuse-prevention purposes. We do not have access to these logs ourselves; they are controlled by GitHub, Inc. and covered by GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Fonts: All fonts used on this site are self-hosted — they are served directly from our own domain rather than from Google Fonts or any other third-party font service. No font-related requests, and no visitor IP addresses, are ever sent to a font provider.

2. How We Use Your Data & Legal Basis

We use the information you provide to:

  • Process and respond to your booking inquiry
  • Contact you regarding your inquiry
  • Communicate event details, terms, and logistics
  • Maintain records of booking inquiries

The legal basis for this processing is (a) your consent, given when you tick the consent checkbox and submit the form, and (b) our legitimate interest / steps necessary to enter into a contract with you (Art. 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR). You may withdraw your consent at any time as described in Section 5.

3. Data Processing & Formspree

Your booking form data is processed by Formspree, a third-party form service. Formspree acts as a data processor on our behalf and complies with GDPR and international data protection standards. Your data is transmitted securely to Formspree's servers, where it is temporarily stored before being delivered to our email address.

International transfer: Formspree, Inc. is based in the United States. Where your data is transferred outside Switzerland/the EEA, Formspree relies on Standard Contractual Clauses and other safeguards recognized under GDPR and Swiss law to ensure an adequate level of protection. For more details, review their privacy policy and Data Processing Addendum.

4. Data Retention

We retain booking inquiry data for as long as necessary to process your inquiry and fulfill the booking (typically 30 days after the event, or longer if required for administrative or legal purposes). You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. Server/hosting connection logs (Section 1) are retained by GitHub under its own retention schedule, which we do not control.

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR and Swiss data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (right to be forgotten)
  • Restriction: Request that we limit how we use your data
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Object: Object to processing based on our legitimate interest
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at siro02@bluewin.ch. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Switzerland, this is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC). If you are located in the EU/EEA, you may instead contact your local data protection authority.

6. Security

Your data is transmitted securely via HTTPS encryption. Formspree uses industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Embedded Content & Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to external services (Instagram, TikTok, SoundCloud). These third parties have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their data practices.

SoundCloud player embeds: The Music section of this site lets you stream tracks via SoundCloud's player. To protect your privacy, the player is not loaded automatically when you visit the page — you'll see a static preview (track title and a play button) that makes no connection to SoundCloud. Only if you click play does your browser load SoundCloud's embedded player (an <iframe> from soundcloud.com), which may then set its own cookies and process your IP address and other technical data according to SoundCloud's Privacy Policy. This connection happens independently of us — we do not receive any data from it. If you prefer to avoid it entirely, you can visit our tracks directly on SoundCloud instead of clicking play on this site.

8. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We do not set any cookies, analytics scripts, pixels, or tracking technology of our own, and we do not use local storage to track you. Two categories of third-party cookies may still reach your browser while using this site, set directly by those third parties and outside our control:

  • Formspree may set cookies while processing your form submission — see their privacy policy for details.
  • SoundCloud may set cookies via the embedded players described in Section 7 — see SoundCloud's privacy policy for details.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Automated Decision-Making

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the website constitutes acceptance of changes.
